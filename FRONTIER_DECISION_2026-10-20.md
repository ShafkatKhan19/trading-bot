# Frontier decision after round 11 (2026-10-20)

Required by round 11's pre-registered stopping rule: with H40–H43
all FAIL, the canonical fundamentals programme is complete — value,
quality, cash-flow, shareholder-yield and insider signals, tested
singly and blended, on large caps and small caps. **45 strategies
tested, 0 pass.**

## What the 45 tests actually established

- The OOS window (2023-07-07 onward) is a powerful mega-cap-led
  bull: SPY +20.6%/yr, Sharpe 1.32. Almost any diversified,
  defensive or non-mega-cap book lags it on raw return.
- Two signals keep producing *positive but statistically weak*
  excess: large-cap earnings yield (H29: beat SPY on CAGR and
  Sharpe, t 0.43) and small-cap value (H33: +11.2%/yr excess,
  t 1.48; H43 blend: +6.2%/yr, t 0.90, control uncorroborated,
  survivorship bias the standing suspect). Blending within one
  universe dilutes (H42 < H29). Momentum is a closed family.
  Calendar anomalies, overnight drift, pairs, low-vol: dead.
- Honest statistical read: with ~3.3 OOS years and monthly
  rebalancing, only a huge effect could clear t ≈ 3.3. The bar
  stays where it is — lowering it would be goalpost-moving — so
  the remaining honest levers are (a) combining *independent*
  weak signals so diversification raises the t-stat if both are
  real, and (b) genuinely new data axes.

## Decision: round 12 runs both levers

1. **H44** — the combination test: 50% large-cap earnings yield
   (H29's exact book) + 50% small-cap value blend (H43's exact
   book), verdict against a 50/50 SPY/IWM blended benchmark. If
   both legs' excesses are real, the combined book's excess
   volatility falls and the t-stat rises; if either is noise, it
   fails like the rest. One test, as registered.
2. **H45** — new data axis, macro regime (FRED, free, public):
   yield-curve switching between SPY and TLT. Cheap to fetch,
   mechanically specified, release-lag handled conservatively.
3. **H46** — defensive/absolute-return design: multi-asset
   inverse-volatility (SPY/TLT/GLD) on cached ETF bars, judged
   against SPY under the same PASS legs as everything else. If
   it fails the excess leg in this bull window — likely — the
   defensive family is on record as tested, not assumed.

Explicitly NOT pursued: lowering the Bonferroni bar, re-tuning
failed factors, intraday strategies (no quality free data; H7
already failed badly), options signals (no free historical
options data of usable quality), and any non-public information
(off-limits, full stop).

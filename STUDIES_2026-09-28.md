# Market studies — SPY, in-sample only (2026-09-28)

Code: `market_studies.py`; full tables: `data/studies/report.txt`.
**Descriptive only.** Nothing here has a verdict or may be traded without its
own pre-registration. Every study uses 2016-01-04 → 2023-07-05, the
in-sample years of all pre-registered tests; the later data stays unseen
for designing ideas. About 60 comparisons are made, so ~3 cells will show
|t| ≥ 2 by chance alone.

| # | Study | What it showed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Daily profile | 46 moves beyond ±3% vs 18 expected under a normal curve. +150% total; +45% without the 10 best days. |
| 2 | Day of week | No weekday effect (all \|t\| < 1.4). |
| 3 | Turn of month | Day +1 of the month +0.23% (t 1.96) vs +0.016% on other days; last day of month -0.05%. Weak, mixed support. |
| 4 | Overnight vs intraday | Roughly even: +65% vs +52% cumulative. No large overnight edge. |
| 5 | VIX regimes | VIX ≥ 30 → next 21 days +5.1% vs ~+1.2% normally; 139 days, mostly 2020. |
| 6 | After big drops | After ≤ -2% days: next day +0.48%, next 21 days +2.3%; 79 cases. |
| 7 | 200-day trend | Below the average: higher mean return but 32% vs 13.5% volatility — why H2 failed. |
| 8 | Intraday shape | No 30-minute window has reliable drift (the one \|t\| > 2 cell is the expected false positive). Volatility highest at open and close. |
| 9 | Costs vs moves | A 0.10% round trip is 67% of a typical 30-minute move, 13% of a day's, 3% of a month's. |

**Takeaways.** Returns are concentrated in a few large days, often right
after the worst ones, which favours holding. Cost drag falls fast with
holding period, which is why every intraday strategy failed and why future
ideas should hold for days or weeks with few trades. Turn-of-month has the
best mix of published evidence, mild in-sample support and fit to this
account's constraints; it is pre-registered next as H8.
